# Crown Labs Portfolio Record

## Canonical purpose

Crown Labs is an applied intelligence studio building interoperable products across intelligence, security, forensics, creative systems, behavioral strategy, and relationship experiences.

This record is the portfolio-level source of truth for the public Crown Labs experience. Product names, readiness labels, valuation ranges, and next gates should remain aligned with the application data model.

## Operating principles

- Evidence-ready outputs over unsupported claims.
- Clear readiness states and explicit next gates.
- Privacy-conscious handling of sensitive inputs.
- Behavioral insight without manipulation.
- Interoperable systems that can stand alone and compound portfolio value.

## Readiness vocabulary

- Concept: defined asset with structure and intent, not yet built.
- Prototype: functional build exists with a scoped feature set.
- Beta: usable by testers, not yet hardened for scale.
- Stage 1 Beta: structured controlled testing against defined readiness criteria.
- Live: publicly available and actively maintained.

## Governance

The landing page is not a separate marketing layer with independent facts. It is part of the same canonical experience as this portfolio record and the product data used to render the portfolio. Changes to product status, positioning, valuation, or next gates should be made at the source of truth and propagated consistently.
